# Hollvik heat pumps: frequently asked questions

Customer FAQ, version 2026-04. For technical details see datasheet TD-LW-2026-03 and installation manual IA-LW-2026-02.

## Is the heat pump loud at night?

The LW series has a night mode that is active from 22:00 to 06:00 by default. In night mode the sound pressure at 3 m distance is 30 dB(A) for the LW 6, 32 dB(A) for the LW 9 and 34 dB(A) for the LW 12. You can change the night mode time window in the Hollvik Home app.

## Does it work in very cold weather?

Yes. All LW models heat down to an outdoor temperature of -25 °C. Below -10 °C the maximum flow temperature is 60 °C. On very cold days the built-in backup heater can support the heat pump.

## Why does the outdoor unit produce steam or stop for a few minutes?

This is the defrost cycle. In cold and humid weather ice forms on the evaporator. The heat pump melts the ice for a few minutes and the warm water vapour can look like steam. This is normal and not a fault. If the display shows E07, the defrost failed; check that the condensate drain is not frozen.

## Can the heat pump cool my house in summer?

The LW 9 and LW 12 can cool through underfloor heating or fan coils. The LW 6 cannot cool. With underfloor cooling the flow temperature does not go below 18 °C to avoid condensation on the floor.

## How do I connect the Hollvik Home app?

Install the app, create an account and select "Add device". Your phone and the HC-2 control unit must be in the same Wi-Fi network. The HC-2 only supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; if your router uses a combined 2.4/5 GHz network name, enable a separate 2.4 GHz network for the setup.

## What happens during a power cut?

The heat pump restarts automatically when the power returns. All settings are kept. If your installer did not install frost protection valves or antifreeze, a long power cut in winter can damage the outdoor unit. Ask your installer how your system is protected.

## Can I use electricity from my solar panels?

Yes. The LW series has an SG Ready input. When your PV system or energy manager signals surplus power, the heat pump raises the hot water and room temperature slightly to store the energy.

## How often does the heat pump need maintenance?

Once a year, by Hollvik service or a certified Hollvik partner. Annual maintenance is also a condition for the 10-year compressor warranty. You can book an appointment online at www.hollvik.example/service or call the service hotline from Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00.

## What should I do when an error code appears?

Note the code and look it up in the error table of the installation manual. Some codes, for example E03 (water flow too low) and E05 (communication error), may be reset once by the owner. If the same code appears again within 24 hours, or if the code cannot be reset by the owner, contact your installer or Hollvik service.

## Who do I contact for a warranty claim?

Contact your installer or Hollvik service at service@hollvik.example. Have the serial number from the type plate and your commissioning protocol ready. Hollvik answers within 2 working days.
