Hollvik LW 6 / LW 9 / LW 12 air-to-water heat pumps

The LW series are monobloc air-to-water heat pumps for outdoor installation. They heat buildings and domestic hot water and use the natural refrigerant R290 (propane). All refrigerant-carrying parts are inside the outdoor unit, so no refrigerant work is needed on site. The indoor control unit HC-2 is included with every heat pump.

7. Connectivity

  • Hollvik Home app for Android and iOS. The HC-2 control unit connects through Wi-Fi in the 2.4 GHz band only; 5 GHz networks are not supported.
  • Modbus TCP interface for building management systems (register list on request).
  • Firmware updates are installed over the air when the unit is online. The installer can disable automatic updates.
