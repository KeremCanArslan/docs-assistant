Manufacturer's warranty for Hollvik heat pumps

This manufacturer's warranty is given in addition to the statutory rights of the buyer against the seller. Those statutory rights are not limited by this warranty.

1. Scope and duration

  • Standard warranty: 5 years on all parts of the heat pump and the HC-2 control unit, starting on the date of commissioning.
  • Extended compressor warranty: 10 years on the compressor, if both conditions in section 2 are met.
  • Labour costs: during the first 2 years Hollvik covers parts, labour and travel costs for warranty repairs. From the third year the warranty covers parts only; labour and travel are charged according to the current service price list.
  • If the commissioning date cannot be proven, the warranty starts on the delivery date shown on the invoice.

2. Conditions for the extended compressor warranty

  1. The unit is registered in the Hollvik partner portal or at www.hollvik.example/register within 60 days of commissioning.
  2. The unit is serviced once a year by Hollvik service or a certified Hollvik partner, and each service is documented in the portal. A gap of up to 15 months between two services is accepted.

3. Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

  • damage caused by installation that does not follow installation manual IA-LW-2026-02, for example missing dirt filter, too small system water volume or incorrect electrical protection;
  • frost damage caused by a power failure when no frost protection valves or antifreeze were installed;
  • corrosion of the evaporator for units installed less than 500 m from the sea coast, unless the coastal protection coating (option KS) was ordered;
  • wear parts such as filter inserts, fuses and seals;
  • damage caused by water quality outside the limits given in the installation manual;
  • cosmetic damage that does not affect the function, for example scratches on the casing.

4. How to make a claim

  1. Contact your installer or Hollvik service (service@hollvik.example) and provide the serial number from the type plate, the commissioning protocol and a description of the fault or the error code.
  2. Hollvik confirms receipt and decides on the next step within 2 working days.
  3. Replaced parts become the property of Hollvik. A warranty repair does not extend or restart the warranty period. Replaced parts have a warranty for the remaining warranty period, but at least 12 months.

5. Change of owner

The warranty stays with the unit when the building is sold. The new owner must update the registration within 30 days of the change of ownership. If the registration is not updated, the standard warranty continues, but the extended compressor warranty ends.

6. Territory

This warranty applies to units installed in Germany, Austria and Luxembourg.
